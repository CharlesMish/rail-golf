# Play beside a line

Open `/lab/places-counterline`. It begins in Solo, in an existing Musical Workshop
arrangement. Fire, watch and listen. When a phrase catches your attention, Keep
it and choose **Use as companion**. Change your aim, elevation, origin or power,
then fire a new physical shot beside that memory.

The companion plays once from the new launch. It is only recorded sound; the
visible ball is the new shot. Try leaving a space, answering a contact, or making
a shorter phrase. A miss or a single note can be useful. Choose **None · Solo
play** whenever you want to compare. Muting each contribution can help you hear
what it adds.

**Overview** shows the arrangement; **Follow / launch** returns to the ordinary
view. **Retry / adjust** stops remaining audio and readies the same world.
**Stop** cancels the pairing. Switching away from the page also stops it; nothing
resumes automatically.

Keep preserves individual physical lines. **Save pairing** preserves two
separate lines with the fixed playback settings. If you change the mix while
playing, Keep the new line and make another pairing with the mix you want.
Download or import lets you move remembered sources between preview addresses.
An imported phrase does not secretly replace your physical world.

Two familiar arrangements and optional recorded examples are available. There
is no need to reproduce a numerical solution. The original Worldcraft editor
and the two-recording Contact Loops listener are linked at the bottom.

As you explore, notice whether the memory gives your next shot a musical reason,
whether live sound feels attached to contact, and whether you prefer changing a
short phrase to searching for a longer one. Those observations matter more than
the number of contacts.
