# Rail Golf — Playability, Persistence & Repetition

**Develop the architectural room and the recoverable persistent object. Keep repeated firing and recorded-contact music as small specialized activities.** Charlie's playtest provides actual enjoyment evidence for the original Cistern and Guest Stone; it does not certify these revisions. The new packet is designed to let that next comparison happen without sacrificing the controls.

## What the playtest changed

The most important finding is that two activities already have an owner who wanted to continue: unexpected journeys through an enclosing room, and purposeful work on a heavy object. The immediate job is to make those activities easier to perceive and continue. Additional architecture, a faster-moving prop or an automatic launcher is valuable only if it serves that job.

Theatre stays byte-for-byte unchanged. Its divided projections were intellectually interesting but less immediately satisfying to the owner. This packet does not add a task or more presentation to defend its originality.

No optional fourth physical concept earned its cost. The finite repeater already combines a new control with the independently proven persistent stone. A sphere/repeater fusion, hinge, waterline, carriage or extra architectural district would add variables before resolving the present comparison.

### Cistern: correct the observation before enlarging the room

The new follow view keeps the ball nearby with a steady world orientation. Overview remains available for context. Rebound-driven camera rotation was rejected because it made obstruction correction abrupt. Camera presentation never changes the accepted trajectory. The original route is still available for comparison.

The 270-case rerun preserves the original 43 exits. High flights can clear the open walls; returning shots can escape across the lower entry side. A centre-segment test found no passage through the interiors of the authored boxes in this sample. That is evidence against a sampled collision hole, not a universal tunneling proof. Closing every exit would erase legitimate architecture and would not fix the distant camera.

The 40.625-second negative also remains reproducible. Its late portion is low-speed rolling on broad stone, including a floor join; removing that join does not cure the tail. There is no hidden drag, wind, sloped floor or altered termination. Stop supplies the missing player decision and labels the result **interrupted**. It does not produce a fake ground event or a normal Keep.

Further architectural development is justified by the owner's enjoyment, but should follow a rendered test of this camera. Broad identifiable regions and useful passages remain the next architectural question. More solids are not the present answer.

### Rolling: more movement is a tradeoff

The selected sphere uses the same mass, diameter, friction, restitution, linear damping and angular damping as the Guest Stone control. Its analytic spherical collision shape differs from the cylinder's convex hull; the visible sphere and painted orientation rings make that distinction honest. An initial lower-angular-damping sphere was rejected for excessive waiting.

The matched central shot moves the sphere about **2.745 m**, versus **0.976 m** for the stone. The sphere is ready at **4.75 s**, versus **1.633 s**. This is meaningful rotation with some sliding, not a claim of perfect no-slip rolling. The same garden provides northward and sideways projects, with bank access and deliberately unsuccessful continuations preserved in the lane evidence.

Ordinary continuation now returns directly to Fire after a measured rest. Keep and Undo remain available until the next blow. A bounded settle failure is not promoted into a valid settled restart. Historical Guest records and its original route remain untouched.

**The original stone remains the stronger default positioning tool** on current evidence: it has direct owner approval and a shorter, more controlled response. The sphere is worth playing as a distinct rolling comparison. Owner evidence of useful placement, rather than admiration of distance, would change that ranking.

### Repeater: a small physical program, not stronger physics

A manually tested launch can be committed for a finite series. The same controls meet the result of the preceding blow. In the retained low-power case, the trial and first repeated blows contact the stone, then the last two attempts miss: its own work moved the object beyond that flight. Stronger or differently approached programs give other progressions.

The queue introduces no impulse or altered contact. Five measured programs match their manually continued controls exactly, record by record. Its agency lies in choosing an action to leave unchanged for a while, then deciding when to cancel or revise it. This is a good specialized experiment; it is not evidence for a tower-defense system, multiple launchers or simultaneous balls.

Every next launch waits for a complete settled state plus a short fixed-tick observation pause. Stop cancels future launches. An already committed attempt finishes; hiding/leaving never queues a silent restart. Trials, repeated attempts and incomplete failures remain distinguishable, with voluntary per-blow Keep and export.

### Contact Loops: retain the rhythm, then listen

The two bundled real lines have three sequential attacks each, at different measured times. A shared cycle preserves those times and adds silence; a half-cycle entrance for the second source is an explicit arrangement operation. Nothing is quantized into a claimed physical contact, and no new simulated ball is required.

This addresses a specific weakness of earlier musical geometry: an interesting rare line need not be repeatedly rediscovered just to hear it again. One source retains its complete phrase in five of six nearby control changes; the other retains none of six. That fragility matters for authoring/discovery, while a correctly recorded history can still replay consistently.

The proof establishes reusable rhythmic material and bounded two-layer playback. It does not establish that these pairings are musically compelling. Owner listening, return visits and voluntary selection of new source lines are the relevant next evidence. Keep this a small listening apparatus, with its source histories visible, rather than turning the physical editor into a sequencer.

Full measured packets: [Cistern](cistern.md), [rolling comparison](rolling.md), [finite repeater](repeater.md), [recorded loops](loops.md). Each links its separate numerical appendix and raw evidence. [Browser and automated verification](BROWSER-AND-CHECKS.md) separates observed results from unavailable rendering.

The sphere reaches the measured north cradle in four blows versus eight stone blows, yet total ready-to-ready physical time is **19.067 s versus 14.283 s**. Fewer clicks is therefore not evidence of a better activity.

## Authoring judgment

| Capability | Proof cost | Playable prototype cost | Generic authoring cost / decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Architectural receiving solids | Existing accepted boxes and real contact probes | Camera, region readability, ending feedback and voluntary interruption | Worth eventual bounded slab/block presets with tested stations and terminal semantics. Camera support is a prerequisite, not an optional finishing detail. |
| Passive persistent prop | One approved collider/material preset | Rest policy, before/end/settled records, Undo/Reset, clear continuation and blocked recovery | Priority candidate, but only a small reviewed preset catalog. Do not expose arbitrary mass, damping, scripts or joints. |
| Finite repeated launch | Small queue around accepted single-shot session | Consent to a finite plan, stop/visibility lifecycle, per-attempt receipts and recovery | Keep specialized. A generic world's persistent-state lifecycle must be validated before it can opt in. |
| Recorded-contact pair | Validated historical attacks through existing voices | Import/provenance, bounded scheduling, start/stop, source comparison | An optional memory/playback tool; it need not change the physical-world schema. |

Physical feasibility, authorability, discoverability, expression and enjoyment remain separate. These are code-owned prototypes; no general imported-world capability was added. Nearby detents show local robustness, and coarse grids show sampled outcomes. Neither establishes human discovery rates. The original owner's enjoyment supports the direction; the new variants still require owner play.

## Recommendation

Prioritize **bounded architectural composition with reliable viewing** and **one recoverable persistent-object template**. Invite a future independent author to make a compact delivery workshop where moving one heavy object changes access to two broad receiving places. Offer the measured stone first and the sphere as an optional deliberate tradeoff; do not prescribe geometry or a scored puzzle.

Keep the repeater opt-in and finite. Promote it only if the owner deliberately chooses a useful fixed program, reacts to its changing consequences, and wants to author another. If it mainly saves repeated clicking, treat it as a convenience rather than a mode. Keep recorded-contact playback if owners find and return to pairings; stop adding arrangement controls if two layers already expose that the material is too sparse or uniform.

Reconsider architecture if even the revised view hides journeys or the room mostly invites Stop. Reconsider the sphere if its waiting outweighs useful roll. Reconsider the persistent template if state restoration remains cumbersome despite simpler continuation. These are reasons to change the design, not to reinterpret passing physics checks as enjoyment.
