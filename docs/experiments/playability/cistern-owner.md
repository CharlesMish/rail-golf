# Visit the Cistern again

Start from the launcher. Aim with the familiar direction, elevation, power and rail controls. The high blue gallery, lower ochre gallery and side chamber, and low green landing offer different places to visit. The dark floor and launch pier end a shot.

The camera now follows the actual ball from nearby and above, keeping the room's orientation steady. During a flight, choose **Overview** to see the whole place, or **Follow ball** to return to its journey. **V** switches those views. Before and after a shot, **Survey** shows the room; the same button returns to the launch view. Retry, Reset and Recall also return to the launcher.

If a line stops being interesting, use **Stop shot**. Its receipt explicitly says interrupted. Stopped attempts cannot be Kept in this version; naturally ended lines use the existing Keep and Restore workflow. Restore prepares the old starting setup; fire manually to run the real shot again.

Try visiting a different region, then returning to a place by another approach. No route is required. The room is open above its walls, and the outer study boundary lies farther away; an escape can be a legitimate outcome.
