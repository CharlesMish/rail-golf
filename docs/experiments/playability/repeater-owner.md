# Visit Shot Repeater

Open `/lab/playability-repeater`. The original Guest Stone garden is your working material: the stone stays where a blow leaves it.

Choose rail, aim, elevation and power, then **Fire once**. After the stone settles, you can fire again immediately, change the launch, or **Start 3–5 shots** using that exact setup. Start continues from the altered garden. It does not restore the trial's starting world.

Watch whether one repeated action keeps doing useful work as the stone moves. A later miss belongs to the experiment too. Try deciding when to trust a short sequence and when to take control again.

**Stop after this blow** is beside the visible stage; it cancels every future shot while the committed ball and stone finish. Between blows it becomes **Cancel remaining shots**. Switching away cancels repetition too. Survey and Closer view change only the camera.

Undo returns to before the last blow. Reset restores home after the active blow finishes. Current attempts keeps the latest trial and up to five separate repeated lines until you fire a new trial or start another sequence. Keep any useful blow or download the individual records first. Restore before and Use result both require a new manual Fire; neither starts a sequence.

This is actual repeated firing, one physical ball at a time. Recorded-contact musical playback is a different experiment.
