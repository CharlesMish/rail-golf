## Selection gates

| Lane | First question | Acceptance gate | Reason to stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cistern | Can a player aim normally and watch the ball's journey? | Opt-in launch/follow/overview camera, measured occlusion, explained exits and explicit interruption; unchanged trajectories for original solids | A more elaborate room hides the ball or mainly prolongs dead clatter |
| Rolling prop | Does rolling create a different useful task? | Actual passive rotation/displacement, sensible wait, held before/settled snapshots, multiple approach intentions and one-step continuation | Long roll or sensitivity outweighs useful positioning |
| Finite repeat | Does a fixed launch act differently on an evolving world? | Manual trial, explicit bounded sequence, one projectile, individual receipts, settle gating, cancellable future attempts | Mostly redundant misses or inconvenient waiting; preserve the negative rather than add progression |
| Contact loops | Can recorded physical timing become useful material? | Two clearly historical audio layers, genuine validated source records, exact event times, explicit start/stop and cleanup | Requires a general sequencer or fictitious physical activity to become interesting |

The optional extra lane is deferred. The rolling/repeater combination is enough of an optional extension if both proofs succeed and their comparison adds a decision. No enemies, waves, economy, arbitrary rigid-body controls or imported scripts are in scope.

## Integrity and cost boundaries

The accepted projectile, station mapping, fixed 120 Hz simulation, gravity and first-ground authority remain unchanged. New passive-prop parameters belong only to a bounded code-owned template. Ball termination, intermediate prop pose, and settled restart state remain distinct. Stopping a flight is not ground contact. Audio-only loops never fabricate fresh Havok events.

Each report distinguishes isolated proof cost, playable UI/lifecycle cost and future generic authoring cost. Generic promotion is not a consequence of a passing fixture. Original routes, physical identities and storage are controls; new behavior is opt-in or on new routes. Keep remains voluntary.

The research browser reports WebGL unavailable on the exact PR81 hosted Cistern. Numerical camera checks are useful but cannot certify rendered readability, clipping through UI, camera comfort or physical-phone performance. DOM, audio controls and non-WebGL presentation can still be checked. Any later successful rendered verification will be recorded explicitly.
