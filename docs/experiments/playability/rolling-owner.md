# Rolling Yard — owner visit

Open `/lab/playability-rolling`. The boulder stays where real impacts leave it. Try moving it toward the north cradle, guiding it into the eastern alcove, or making space for a different line through the yard.

Choose a rail, aim, elevation and power, then Fire. Watch the painted rings as well as the ball. The ball stops on pavement; the boulder gets time to settle. When Fire returns, the altered world is already ready for your next shot. You do not need to choose a continuation command.

Use Survey / Closer view to inspect the place. On a phone, “Shot controls” returns from the stage to the controls. Sound can be muted. Undo last blow lets you try a different choice from the same earlier world; Reset yard returns the object home.

The Comparison selector switches between the round boulder and the original stone's shape and physics in this same yard. Switching resets the yard; each has its own kept blows. The untouched Guest Stone remains available through the footer link.

Keep a blow when its physical line or resulting world matters. Inside Kept blows, “Restore before” prepares the original starting state; “Use result” prepares the settled result. Both require a fresh manual Fire. A failed settling attempt explicitly pauses and offers Undo/Reset; a paused moving pose is not a completed saved result.

See whether the round object suggests a different kind of work, and whether its larger movement is worth the extra settling time. Neither destination is compulsory. A once-useful shot may need revision after the boulder moves.
