# Chime Garden

Four timber banks and eight metal plates stand in a clearing. The pieces stay where they are. Aim, elevation, and power can change. There is no circle and no score. The line ends when the ball first meets the ground.

Try different parts of the clearing. A shot that only rings one surface can be walked, a small step at a time, until more surfaces answer. Some aims ring nothing. A small change of aim can ring a different group of surfaces.

The opening aim is one place to start. It is not the only one.
