# Chime Terraces

Three timber ramps stand in front of the tee. Behind them, three rows of metal plates rise and lean back toward the tee. The rows are pitched so a path that moves down a column changes note. The pieces stay where they are. Origin, yaw, elevation, and power can change inside the rail stops. There is no circle and no score.

The line ends on first ground, or when the ball leaves the yard. Survey shows the three heights from the side. The note line under the shot names the plates that sounded, in order.

The printed aim is only a place to start. Other aims inside the stops reach other pieces. A small change of aim often keeps a run of notes and moves it onto a neighbouring plate.
