# Chime Fan

Three banks stand across the clearing. Plates hang above the far side of each one. The pieces stay where they are. There is no circle and no score. The line ends when the ball meets the ground.

The aim that opens the world rings one bank. Moving the aim to the left or the right rings a different bank. Raising the aim can carry the ball over a bank and into the plates and the far piece behind it. A small step on a slider often keeps the same pieces and changes how many times they answer.

The bank sound control is the existing timber voice. Metal plates and the far banks stay the long metal voice. Sound does not change the flight.

Open it from the Foundry, under Chime Terraces.
