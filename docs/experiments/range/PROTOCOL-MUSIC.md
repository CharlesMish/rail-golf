# Owner protocol — Musical Workshop

One sitting on `/lab/worldcraft`. Do not open [MUSIC.md](MUSIC.md), [appendix/music/](appendix/music/), or [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) during the sitting.

The **Range** menu is in the header. Nothing on this route is a production score.

Write the same two lines you use for the other experiments, after you decide you are done:

1. After the shot you were willing to keep, or after you decide you are done trying, did you change another piece or fire again?
2. When you are offered to stop, did you stop or keep experimenting?

The offer to stop is a sentence, said out loud: "You can stop." Say it once, after the first shot whose impact line you can read, and wait long enough to notice what you do next.

## Enter

**Range** → **Musical Workshop**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

Fixed Shot Workshop is the other workshop. Its shot stays locked, so a piece change is the only difference between two trails. This one lets the shot move too. Resonant Phrase is still the fixed-shot challenge. It is not this sitting.

## What you can change

The ramp, the bank, and the plate. Origin, yaw, elevation, and power, inside the rail stops. Movable pieces are tinted, and the piece list says movable. Nothing in this preset is fixed.

## Fire

Build, then **Play →**, then **Fire line**. The line under the button is the sounds that shot actually made, in contact order. It is not a score and not a rhythm. A piece that was not touched is named. A piece with no voice does not ring. A later hit on a piece that already rang is its own note.

**Ready again** keeps this world and this aim. **Back to Build** is where a piece moves.

The amber dashed trail is the previous recorded flight. The cyan trail is the one you just fired.

## Keep

**Keep line** stores the world, the note order, and the contact times. **Hear recorded sounds** plays that recording. It does not fire the shot again.

Then write the two lines. Then the offer to stop.
