# Owner protocol — Musical Workshop, second sitting

One sitting on `/lab/worldcraft`. Do not open [MUSIC-2.md](MUSIC-2.md), [MUSIC.md](MUSIC.md), or [appendix/music2/](appendix/music2/) during the sitting.

Use the same two lines as the other experiments, after you decide you are done:

1. After the shot you were willing to keep, or after you decide you are done trying, did you change another piece or fire again?
2. When you are offered to stop, did you stop or keep experimenting?

The offer to stop is a sentence, said out loud: "You can stop." Say it once, after the first shot whose note timeline you can read, and wait long enough to notice what you do next.

## Enter

**Range** → **Musical Workshop**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

This sitting is the workshop only. Back Pocket, Soft Landing, Resonant Phrase, and Travelling Bell are not part of it.

## Fire twice

Build, then **Play →**, then **Fire line**. **Ready again**, then **Fire line** again.

The amber dashed trail is the previous recorded flight. The cyan trail is the one you just fired. On the amber trail, each contact that rang is a marker labeled with its note and the contact time.

Under the impact line, **This shot** and **Previous shot** list those notes and the elapsed time between them.

The line is still not a score and not a rhythm.

## Keep

**Keep line** stores the world, the note order, and the contact times. **Hear recorded sounds** plays that recording. It does not fire the shot again.

Then write the two lines. Then the offer to stop.
