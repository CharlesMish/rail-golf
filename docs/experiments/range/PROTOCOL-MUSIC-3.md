# Owner protocol — Musical Workshop, bank voice

One sitting on `/lab/worldcraft`. The shot is unchanged by the bank sound. This sitting is to hear the bank, not to score a line.

## Enter

**Range** → **Musical Workshop**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

The bank sound starts on **H · G, grazes lifted**. That is the provisional default. A and B are still in the same menu. Back Pocket, Soft Landing, Resonant Phrase, and Travelling Bell are not part of this sitting.

## What to listen for

Fire one soft bank contact and one squarer bank contact. Then fire a line that rings more than the bank. After each, switch the bank sound and use **Replay last shot**. Replay does not fire again. The trail and the note timeline stay the ones just recorded.

Listen for three things:

1. The bank under H against the same contact under A, then under B. A is the short knock. B is that knock with the softer contacts brought up. H is the longer wooden note, and light contacts sit higher than B.
2. Whether a timber ramp or a timber plate, if you retune one, still sounds like ordinary timber when H is selected. Only the bank should take the long note.
3. Whether Replay last shot, and a line you already kept, follow the sound now selected. Keeping a line does not freeze the sound it was kept under.

Do not treat a match of loudness as a musical judgement. Write down only whether the bank is the piece that changed.
