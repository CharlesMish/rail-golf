# Owner play protocol

One sitting on `/lab/worldcraft`. The **Range** menu is in the header. Nothing on this route is a production score. Do not open [appendix/](appendix/) during the sitting. Do not open [REPORT.md](REPORT.md).

Write two lines in a notebook after each experiment, before the next one:

1. After the success condition (or after you decide you are done trying), did you change another piece or fire again?
2. When you are offered to stop, did you stop or keep experimenting?

The offer to stop is a sentence, said out loud: "You can stop." Say it yourself, or have someone else say it. Say it once, after the first note, and wait long enough to notice what you do next.

## Open Workshop

Enter: **Range** → **Open Workshop**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

Fire the shot that is already set. Move or retune one piece. Fire the same shot again. The earlier trail is the one that was recorded. Success is seeing that the piece change changed the shot. There is no circle and no target.

Then write the two lines.

## Back Pocket

Enter: **Range** → **Back Pocket**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

The carriage stays put. Aim and power stay inside the window shown beside the goal. You may move the spare bank. The timber screen stays. Success is the first ground contact inside the gold circle.

Try. When you land in the circle, or when you decide to stop trying, write the two lines. The offer to stop comes after that, even if you have not landed in the circle.

## Soft Landing

Enter: **Range** → **Soft Landing**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

The gold circle stays. You may move the block, move the bank, and change origin, aim, and power inside the rail stops. Success is the round's centre inside the circle at first ground, on a shot you can set up again.

Try to make a landing you can repeat. Then write the two lines. Then the offer to stop.

## Resonant Run

Do Free first, then Phrase. Same two lines after each. The offer to stop comes after Phrase, not between them, unless you want to stop after Free. If you stop after Free, write that down.

### Free

Enter: **Range** → **Resonant Run: Free**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

Fire the shot that is already set. Move or retune a piece. Fire that same shot again. Success is firing the same shot after a change you can hear or see. There is no required order.

### Phrase

Enter: **Range** → **Resonant Run: Phrase**. Reset: **Reset preset**.

The order on the goal note is C4, then D4, then E4. The stand-in is a separate hit, not one of those notes. Success is that order in the contact record. It is not a score.

## Travelling Bell (optional)

Skip this if the sitting is already long. If you skip it, write "skipped" and do not treat that as the offer-to-stop note.

Enter: **Range** → **Travelling Bell**. Reset: **Reset preset**. Reset also puts the release phase back to 0.

The pieces stay. The lever is the release phase. Success is a contact with the bell before the line ends. First ground still ends the line.

Change the phase and fire. When you get a bell contact, or when you decide to stop trying, write the two lines. Then the offer to stop.
