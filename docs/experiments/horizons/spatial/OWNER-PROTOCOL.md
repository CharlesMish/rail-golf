# Overpass — owner entry

Open **Overpass** from the Horizons index or Worldcraft's Foundry list. Survey the roof, the open passage below it and the wall beyond. The structure is fixed; origin, yaw, elevation and power are yours. There is no target or score.

1. Fire the opening setup and follow where the ball first touches the structure.
2. Try making the same place behave differently: go below it, use its top, or pass it entirely. Change one control at a time when something surprises you.
3. Once you like a line, vary its destination or return distance. Keep it if you want to remember it; Restore should let you revisit the same starting world and setup.

Please notice whether the roof gives you a clear next idea, whether top/underside/edge contacts are distinguishable, and whether the side result view helps you follow the line. On a phone, check the geometry and controls in both orientations. The developer could run real physics but could not render WebGL here.

Stop whenever it starts feeling like searching blindly for numbers. That is useful negative evidence; this is a short spatial study, not a test you have to solve.
