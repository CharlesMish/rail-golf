# Radial Return

Open **Foundry → Featured worlds → Radial Return → Play**. This is a short physical study: a wall beyond two shelves at different heights, viewed from a forward launch station.

A direct preview ending in `?foundry=radial-return` opens this same study without firing. **Center Survey** returns to its overview.

Fire the opening shot and follow its outward and inward legs. Then change one control at a time. The origin, aim, elevation and power remain yours; the three pieces stay fixed. See whether you can make a returning line use a different shelf, pass above one, or leave the arrangement entirely.

Keep any line you want to revisit, and use **Restore world & line** to compare a revision. There is no score or required contact sequence. Ground still ends the shot.

The useful owner questions are whether the return is easy to follow, whether catching or skipping a shelf feels like a choice, and whether one result gives you an idea for another shot. The [technical appendix](RESULTS.md) contains numerical solutions and measured failure cases.

Physics is verified. Browser WebGL was unavailable in this session, so desktop/phone framing and enjoyment remain unverified.
