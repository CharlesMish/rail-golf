## Open it

Open the preview. In the header, choose **Foundry**. Under **Featured** there are two new entries:

- **Descending Resonance**. Six of its plates hang in the air and lean. This is the experiment.
- **Descending Resonance — Flat**. Everything stands on the ground. This is the control.

Press **Play** on one. **Reset** puts it back. Nothing in either world can be moved. Aim, elevation, power, and the carriage position are yours. There is no target and no score. The line ends when the ball meets the ground, or when it settles.

Thin pale lines under a hanging plate show where it is over the ground and how far up it starts. They are marks. The ball passes through them.

When a shot ends, the view moves to one side so you can see the heights. **Address** takes you back behind the launcher.

## A session

About twenty-five minutes, in this order. The order matters: it puts the new one first, while your ear is fresh, and the one you already know last.

1. **Descending Resonance, ten minutes.** Play it as an instrument. Start anywhere. When a shot makes something you like, do not repeat it. Move one control a little and fire again. Do that several times before you move on. Try low shots and high ones, soft and hard.
2. **Descending Resonance — Flat, five minutes.** The same way.
3. **Ricochet Garden — Loop, five minutes.** The same way.
4. **Back to whichever you want.** Notice which one that was.

Play at least part of it on a phone, with sound on.

## What to notice

While you play, not afterwards:

- After a line you liked, what did the next small change give you? The same line, a different line, or nearly nothing?
- How long did it take to get a line of three or more notes without trying to?
- Did the lines sound different from each other, or like one line with small changes?
- Could you tell, from watching, why a shot did what it did?
- Could you see the hanging plates well enough to aim at them? From behind the launcher, and on a phone?
- Was any shot too long, or too busy to follow by ear?
- Did it still feel like taking a shot?

## Afterwards

- Which of the three did you go back to?
- Between the two new ones, did the hanging plates matter to you, or was the flat one already the improvement?
- Did any line surprise you?
- Was there a note you heard too often?
- Would you want to place hanging plates yourself?

The last question decides the most. If the flat one already does it, nothing about the world format needs to change. If the hanging plates are what you kept coming back for, the next step is controls for placing them.
