# The Other Way

Spoiler-free notes for the wildcard lane. Passing aims, windows, and the ridge between a stop and a carry are in [SOLUTIONS.md](SOLUTIONS.md). The gallery invitation does not state them.

Kit SHA this lane was cut from: `1ad3166d0839ea042379f5a861ed7523cb46d47e`. Physics was not changed. `sourceBuild` in the manifest is that SHA.

## Concept

Three dull blocks stand in a row across the clearing. They are fixed. Nothing can be added. There is no circle, no bell, and no phrase.

The opening shot meets the middle block and stops in front of it. That line is already finished. It is not a mistake, and nothing is scored.

The reason to fire again is that one finished line can spend only one commitment. A way past the blocks is still unused. Taking one way leaves the others. The next shot is not there to correct a miss, to learn a rebound, to place a piece, or to make a tune.

This does not fit the other lanes:

- It is not a physical-discovery study. The play is not an unusual rebound, a return line, or a hidden angle. The blocks are dull, so meeting one stops the shot instead of teaching a bounce.
- It is not a construction puzzle. The player does not add or move a piece. The three blocks stay.
- It is not musical play. No piece has a voice.
- It is not the travelling-bell timing study. Nothing moves. That earlier study lost its whole phase window under a 0.5° aim change. This opening does not.

The player can simply play. Origin, aim, and power are free, on the existing slider steps. Make a copy saves a separate world. It does not change this arrangement.

## What the opening is for

The opening is the stop. It shows that the middle block is solid, and it does not itself pass the blocks. The ways past are left for later shots. After a stop, a left, a right, and a carry, the study has answered its question. How far a clear line lands can still change with power. That is a little more play, not a second puzzle.

## What was checked

Headless Havok, 1153 shots, seed 20261109, zero rejected setups. The sweep is `sweep.mjs`. The concise record is `results.json`.

The opening stop survived one real slider step in yaw, elevation, charge, and origin. Each way past that was kept as an example also survived those same one-step neighbours. The families are many slider steps wide. A thinner ridge, where the ball clips the top of the middle block and still gets past, is written up as a specialist result in the solutions file. It is not the opening and not the invitation.

No goal is bypassed, because there is no goal. The straight carry is one of the measured ways, kept because the blocks are 4 m tall and cannot be raised. Locked pieces and an empty allowance mean a player cannot delete the middle block.

## Limitations, not built

- A goal cannot bless more than one landing without calling the others misses. This world has no goal.
- The ground has no mark for either side, and a saved copy does not keep the invitation sentence.
- A taller dull face would remove the straight carry. The block has no height control, so the carry stays.
