# Bell Loft: measured appendix

Evidence for the foundry world `worlds/foundry/bell-loft`. The validator replays the five tested shots in the manifest on the authored arrangement. Everything below that needs the fourth wall moved is recorded here and is **not** replayed by the validator.

Kit: `lab/wc-foundry-20261109` at `1ad3166d0839ea042379f5a861ed7523cb46d47e`. No physics, catalog, or runtime file was changed. All runs are headless Havok through `scripts/range/run.mjs` (the validator's harness), 120 Hz, seed 20261109 recorded as provenance.

## The physical relationship

A block's top is 4 m up and is not ground. Its dull coefficients (friction 0.8, restitution 0.1) take most of the bounce out of a landing. A round that lands there keeps rolling, because nothing on a flat top slows a roll. Four 12 m banks around two blocks make a 12 × 5 m room whose walls stand 3 m above that floor. Once all four walls are up, the round rattles between them, slows, and the session ends the line as `settled` at y 4.43. It never reaches first ground.

With one wall standing aside, the same lines land upstairs, ring the back bell, and roll out of the open front to first ground about 4 m in front of the loft. That is the opening a player sees.

## The open front never holds

On the authored arrangement, 1,485 further shots were fired: origin x −10 to 10 in 2 m steps, yaw −16° to 10° in 1° steps, and five aims (50°/0.50, 52°/0.51, 30°/0.57, 24°/0.65, 12°/0.49). None settled. The door is the one relationship that decides a catch.

So a catch sounds like a wooden knock, one or two clear bells, and near-silence while the round crawls to rest. The long rattle is visible in the ledger but is almost inaudible. This is recorded as a limitation in the manifest. No listening test was run.

## Browser check

Headless Chrome (SwiftShader) against the dev server at desktop width, then 390 px for the gallery. The card lists Bell Loft after Soft Landing with its name, invitation, "Authored world · Launcher editable · Geometry editable", "Optional challenge", Play, Reset, and Make a copy. Play loaded the preset in Survey. The opening ended on first ground with the same terminal as headless (5.191666666666714 s, z 45.49125671386719). Dropping the door at x 3 was refused ("overlaps another piece"). At x −4, z 49 it was accepted, and the unchanged shot ended `settled` at 15.249999999999774 s, y 4.43. Reset put the door back at x 9. Make a copy saved "Copy of Bell Loft" as its own world. No console errors. Screenshots show that the follow camera hides the caught round behind the walls (`caught-address.png`), while Survey shows the whole line (`caught-survey.png`).
