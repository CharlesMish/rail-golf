# Return Address

Play catch across the launch deck. Send a ball down the clearing and bring it home to the other half of the deck. Start your reply from where it landed.

1. On this branch, run the existing local app with `npm run dev`. Open `/lab/worldcraft`, choose **Foundry**, then **Return Address → Play**. Survey opens on the yard.
2. Fire the opening line. Look at the last patch of its trail. Which side would you stand on to receive it? Did it reach the deck? Change one thing and fire again.
3. When a ball comes home, move **Origin** to the place you think it touched the deck. Throw back toward the first sender. A reply that drifts gives you another place to play from.

A catch is yours to call. The tee is the dark launch deck. **Contact record** gives the first touch coordinates for each body, including the deck. Its first coordinate is the position across the rail in metres. **Survey** can show both recent trails. **Keep line** saves a throw you like.

The tinted face can move in Build. **Make a copy** keeps an arrangement under **Worlds & lines**. **Reset** on the Foundry card restores the authored opening.

For an owner playtest, the question is whether seeing one landing makes you choose the next launch position yourself. Try that before opening the [technical appendix](EVIDENCE.md).
