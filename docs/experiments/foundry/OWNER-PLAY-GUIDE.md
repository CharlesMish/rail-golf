# Foundry gallery

Worldcraft is the lab at `/lab/worldcraft`. Foundry is the button in the header. It opens a short list of prepared worlds. Nothing on the card is a solution.

The list has three groups:

- **Featured:** Bell Loft, Return Address, Right Angle, Open Contacts.
- **Construction lesson:** The Carry.
- **Short studies:** The Other Way, and Soft Landing.

Each card shows the invitation, the kind of world, and whether the launcher and the pieces can be edited. **Play** opens that arrangement. **Reset** puts the prepared arrangement back and leaves a saved copy alone. **Make a copy** saves a new world on this browser. The original stays as it was.

On a phone the invitation sits with the fire control, so it stays in view. On a wider screen it is the first sentence of the card at the top of the side panel. The next sentence is read from the world that is open: whether the shot is locked, which kinds of piece can actually move, and whether a gold circle is judging the first landing. A locked world is not told to revise a locked piece.

## What each world invites

**Bell Loft.** A stone loft stands in the clearing. Three of its walls are bells; the fourth stands aside. The ground ends a line it touches. The loft floor is not the ground. The shot can change, and so can that fourth wall and the wooden stair. If a shot is still in the air after a few seconds, the status says it is still settling. If the ball comes to rest up on a raised piece, Survey frames that piece and the receipt says so in plain words.

**Return Address.** Play catch across the launch deck. Send a ball down the clearing and bring it home to the other half of the deck. Start the reply from where it landed. When a shot touches the deck, the receipt names that contact and the nearest carriage stop, and it says the next launch starts from there. You still move the carriage.

**Right Angle.** Two banks meet at a right angle, off to the right of the clearing. The first shot runs straight past them. The banks stay put. Aim and power are yours. A shot that touches both faces and comes back is shown in Survey. The receipt names both faces and how far the ball came back toward the carriage.

**Open Contacts.** A timber bank faces the tee. Metal plates stand to the sides and farther down the clearing. There is no target. The shot can change, and the bank can be moved. This world uses the existing bank-sound control and the existing note timeline. The card keeps the name Open Contacts.

**The Carry.** The shot is locked. A gold circle sits down the clearing, and one ramp starts on the near ground. The card says the printed shot stays, and that the ramp can move. The circle judges the first landing. The landing is not a score.

**The Other Way.** Three blocks stand across the clearing. The opening shot meets the middle one and stops. A way past them is still unused, and taking one leaves the others. The pieces stay. After the opening, the status does not ask you to revise a piece. Aim and power can change.

**Soft Landing.** A gold circle sits down the clearing. A block and a bank start off to the side. The shot can change, and so can those two pieces. This is the worked example that was already in the kit.

## Reset and copy

**Reset**, on the card or beside the world name, reloads the prepared preset. A copy you already saved is a different world. **Make a copy** names it “Copy of …” and stores it with your other saved worlds. Export and import stay where they were, under Worlds & lines.
