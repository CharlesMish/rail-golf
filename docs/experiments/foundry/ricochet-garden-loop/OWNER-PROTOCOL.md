# Ricochet Garden — Loop, for Charles

Pinned preview: the hash-prefixed `*.workers.dev` URL for this commit, at `/lab/worldcraft`. It is filled in after Cloudflare Workers Builds publishes the commit. It is not the branch alias, and it is not `rail-golf.cmish.dev`.

## Open it

Open that preview. In the header, choose **Foundry**. Under **Featured**, open **Ricochet Garden — Loop** and press **Play**. **Reset** puts the prepared arrangement back. **Make a copy** saves a new world on this browser and leaves the prepared one alone.

The card already says what the clearing is: plates and two banks partly close it, with an opening toward the tee. The shot can change, and one bank can be turned. There is no target and no score. The line ends when the ball meets the ground, or when it settles.

Play it as an instrument. Improvise. Nothing on the card is a solution, and this note does not add one.

## What to listen and look for

After a shot, read the contact sequence. The impact line and the note timeline are the order the pieces actually sounded, with the gap between notes. Listen for a hit near the end or the top of a piece, for a rebound onto a second piece, and for a ball that comes back up the clearing. The bank sound control is the one already used by Musical Workshop. It does not change the flight.

## A session

Give it about ten minutes of free play. Change aim and power however you like, and fire again when you want to hear the next one.

Then turn the one bank the card says can move. Fire a few more shots. **Reset** if you want the prepared bank back.

Then open **Foundry** again and play **Open Contacts** for a few minutes. It is the earlier open yard. Come back to Loop if you want to compare them in the same sitting.

## Afterwards

- Did you want another shot for its own sake?
- Which shot surprised you?
- Did moving the bank matter?
- On a phone and on a desktop, could you read the contact sequence?
- Open Contacts or this one: which did you want to keep playing?
