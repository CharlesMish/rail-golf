# Foundry solutions

This page is the appendix. The gallery cards do not show it. Setups below are the tested shots in each manifest. The independent review’s measurements and contact tables are in [Solutions and evidence](https://github.com/CharlesMish/rail-golf/blob/c080b42a8e31d723648791e6ab7ef43212df9ccb/docs/experiments/foundry/review/REVIEW.md#solutions-and-evidence) on `c080b42`.

Launcher numbers are origin x in metres, yaw in degrees, elevation in degrees, and charge from 0 to 1. A layout is a move of a piece that is already in the preset. Contacts are the ledger order, with the ground left out.
