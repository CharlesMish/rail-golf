# A visit to Places & Counterlines

Open the pinned `/lab/places` preview linked in the draft PR/publication ledger.
There is no required order, score or unlocking step. Numerical solutions are in
separate appendices; none are needed for this visit.

## Exchange Yard

The Forecourt faces a railway crossing. The Service lane looks across the works.
Both are freely available. Look at the passage, deck and rooftops, then choose
one relationship you want to investigate. Change a single control and watch what
becomes reachable. Survey reads the place; Follow watches the actual ball.

The dark street ends a shot. Raised buildings, platforms and pitched roofs are
physical. Fine tracks, windows and brick courses are painted markings. Stop is
available when a journey has finished being interesting. Keep a line you want to
revisit; Restore brings back its station and setup but never fires.

Does another region catch your attention after the first interesting shot? Does
changing station suggest a different intention? Please note any camera movement
that hides or interrupts the relationship you wanted to watch.

## Counterline

Start Solo in Musical Workshop. Fire and Keep a phrase that interests you—even a
short one. Select it as the companion, change the next shot, then Fire again.
The remembered phrase plays once alongside the new physical contacts. Only the
new ball is simulated.

Try listening to both parts, or mute either contribution. Return to Solo whenever
you want. A recorded example is available, but discovering your own phrase is
the central experiment. Save pairing retains the two physical histories and a
fixed mix; changing mutes mid-performance leaves the individual line Keepable but
requires a new fixed-mix performance before saving a pairing.

Stop, Retry, a new launch or changing companions cancels the old playback. A
naturally finished ball can leave the companion sounding to its end. Restore,
import and reload remain silent. Download material you want to carry between
preview addresses; browser shelves are local to each preview's origin.

Does the companion give you an idea for the next shot—an answer, a gap to fill or
a contrast? Does the sound feel connected to the live ball? Both parts are slightly
buffered for synchronization; focus loss or a long stall stops the pairing.
Older imported lines preserve notes/timing but may not reproduce their original
voice selection exactly.

## Direct repeats

Choose **Fire once** or commit **Repeat × 3/4/5** directly. The number counts total
new shots. The aim stays fixed while the stone's position changes. Each next shot
waits for an actual settled state. Cancel stops remaining launches; an already
committed blow finishes.

Inspect or Keep individual attempts, Undo a blow or Reset the garden. The linked
manual-trial version remains available for comparison. Preserve a batch before
starting another if you want its individual records.

## Earlier activities remain available

Gallery Cistern, Rolling Yard, Guest Stone, the original Repeater, Contact Loops
and unrestricted Worldcraft remain intact. This session does not ask you to
choose one universal game mode.

The 3D activities require WebGL. The research browser could inspect controls and
measure audio but could not render their physical scenes; your camera and musical
feel observations will close that important gap.
