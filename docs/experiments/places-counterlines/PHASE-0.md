## Evidence gates

Actual Havok establishes physical reachability, collisions, nearby variation and
repeatability. The atlas samples explicit legal control settings; it is not a
model of human play frequency. A simple two-contact line may be more useful than
a long clatter. Architectural regions, launch bands and distinct intentions matter
more than contact totals.

Audio tests must distinguish preserved event time, scheduling lateness, acoustic
latency and rendered visual time. Natural musical completion may outlive the new
ball; explicit interruption must retire stale playback. Restore/reload never
starts a performance. Browser limitations, NullEngine timings and unmeasured
owner enjoyment must remain explicit.

Independent review, repository checks and durable publication accompany the
playable lanes. Numerical solutions belong in technical appendices, away from
the invitation and spoiler-free owner guide.
