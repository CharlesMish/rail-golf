# Instrument Foundry & Expressive Geometry — director handoff

The primary outcome is a usable invitation to independent instrument authors:
a small validated instrument file, a shared audition player, two exact references,
a reproducible Havok probe command and a portable starter packet. It is ready for
an authoring round without making curved geometry or the roof repair dependencies.
Publication endpoints, draft PRs and immutable previews are in `PUBLICATION.md`;
the final draft receipt records its own exact HEAD outside the commit.

## Design judgment

The owner's ensemble is stronger evidence than an optimized demonstration. Its
three independent physical performances reproduce exactly in equivalent fresh
Havok scenes, and the owner has already enjoyed using them together. The two
Descending sources have different musical relationships; the live source revisits
a plate and wall, giving five musical impacts but three qualified sport redirects.
Terraces contributes four substantial impacts plus one extremely small positive
event. We preserve the actual history without promoting the tiny event into an
equally meaningful heard note.

This supports treating a world as a physical instrument, with useful material
that can be incomplete on its own. A short answer, a pause, or a repeated return
can be more useful than an independently complete tune. It does not establish
that every playable world belongs in the musical repertoire.

The first authoring round should emphasize **spatial arrangement and accessible
variation with the existing pieces**. Origin matters: all three owner sources
use the lowest elevation and deliberate origin changes, a region a central-only
launch survey would miss. Do not prescribe an amphitheater, a terrace or an
artificial musical role to each model. Give them the same working contract and
let physical ideas emerge before deciding which instruments complement one another.

## What the primary foundation provides

| Capability | Status and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Banks, ramps, blocks, voiced plates | Existing static Worldcraft vocabulary; unchanged geometry and physics |
| Plate lift/tilt and capacity24 | Existing validated extensions reused; no new free scaling or mesh import |
| Instrument invitation, curated opening, acoustic interpretation, Overview camera | New small versioned envelope; bounded data, no executable content/assets |
| Shared audition | New route reuses the existing solo/duet/trio Counterline implementation |
| Recorded companions and Keeps | Existing independent physical records and acoustic sidecars; unchanged formats/storage bounds |
| Reviewed registration | Small data-list seam; two exact references plus six additional registered/session slots |
| Author diagnostic | New strict actual-Havok CLI with ordinary neighboring controls, misses and cleanup reports |
| Curved element | Demonstrated separately; not an approved instrument JSON kind or portable companion source |
| Concavity, arbitrary meshes, new registers/timbres | Deferred; require a concrete expressive need and separate engineering review |

The two references keep their ordered physical documents, original and curated
starts, pitches and profiles. Ten representative full histories match the old
adapters exactly. The new wrapper adds zero physics bodies, meshes or materials.
There is no new physics, recording or audio-scheduling implementation. No scripts,
remote assets or unrestricted coefficients can enter through instrument files.

One inherited Restore ambiguity was corrected: an explicitly historical source
with no captured acoustic interpretation restores canonical total-speed behavior,
rather than silently inheriting today's matched registry profile. Captured sidecars
remain authoritative. Historical formats and bytes are not rewritten, and exact
old timbre is not promised where it was never stored.

## Evidence and limits

`reference-evidence.md` distinguishes five candidate families per reference,
ordinary neighbors, sampled misses and broad relationship preservation from exact
whole-phrase repetition. The two stable Descending plate descents are different
regions/pitches of one physical type, not two new mechanics. Terraces often changes
its precise notes while retaining the route through upper/middle/lower regions.
The 135-setting surveys include 57 silent Descending and 53 silent Terraces shots;
they are not human play frequencies and do not prove effortless discovery.

The author validator completes 58 and 59 real reference shots with clean resets
and disposal. An independent reviewer unpacked the actual starter, changed only
JSON and completed 26 shots using the exact kit checkout. That sparse fixture is
a workflow test, not a third creative submission. Separate imported-world probes
preserve identical full physical histories with zero, one and two companions and
round-trip sources after the session catalog is gone.

The inherited Descending construction-history/seam sensitivity remains disclosed.
Equivalent fresh initialization is the replay boundary, not a universal exactness
promise after arbitrary prior world switches. Authors should avoid coincident
surfaces and report construction-history variation when relevant.

The research browser cannot initialize WebGL. Real interface import/rejection,
source selection, scrolling and keyboard access were inspected, but no rendered
world, listening result, GPU cleanup or phone frame-rate claim is made. NullEngine
CPU timings and resource counts are useful engineering evidence, not rendered
performance. See `VERIFICATION.md` for exact checks and boundaries.

## Separate roof repair

The old gables reversed triangle indices after flat shading left Babylon on an
unindexed draw path. Both main and shadow passes ignored those indices and
submitted inward triangles. The working barrel corrected order before flattening.
Selecting indexed drawing for the three gables uses the already-correct outward
indices without altering any vertex, normal, index buffer, collider or identity.
All 32 complete control histories remain exact. Independent review reproduces
the causal defect. The comparator preserves the old recipes.

This is a verified source/draw-dispatch correction, not a declaration that every
owner-visible artifact has disappeared. Two-sided improvement fits the diagnosis;
remaining shadow/cap symptoms still require rendered comparison. The canopy and
Freight remain intact, with no construction added to justify Freight.

## Optional curve: retain the question, defer the generic kind

Across the crown rotates the established ten-facet barrel across flight, using
one honest convex hull and two receiving decks. A repeated-crown line creates two
separated actual onsets but one sport redirect; a low carry changes destination
relative to its flat control. Four selected relationships retain their core in
all six ordinary neighbors. Near return and high carry also work flat, and the
flat control has broader sampled coverage. Long low-energy repetitions are
preserved as negatives.

This is worth an optional owner visit. It is not yet a reusable Counterline
instrument: its code-owned spatial records are deliberately incompatible with
the ordinary instrument record contract. That boundary avoids disguising a new
collider as an old v1 box. If contact-position choice and separated returns prove
expressive in play, the next extension should be one bounded barrel element with
explicit snapshot/identity/camera/export support. Concave and arbitrary mesh
geometry would add considerable seam and authoring burden before that evidence.

## Conducting the independent round

Send the same pinned starter and `CONTRIBUTOR-BRIEF.md` to each model. Ask for one
considered instrument, probe file, honest evidence and a spoiler-free invitation.
Keep changes in a unique instrument/evidence directory and use data imports for
audition; authors need no registry or engine changes. A short concept note can
identify missing capabilities before substantial engineering work.

Review data validity, physical evidence, owner discovery and musical usefulness
as separate decisions. First play each submission without its numerical appendix,
then answer one retained reference phrase and compare it with another submission.
Choose genuinely distinct contributions; do not fill six slots merely because
six are available. Register selected files in the bounded catalog only after that
review, preserving each version's provenance. `INTEGRATION.md` gives the procedure.

Evidence that would change this recommendation includes several independently
invented concepts blocked by the same small geometric or acoustic limit, owner
preference for a curved study over its flat control, or repeated failure to find
useful phrases despite validated accessible routes. In that case revise the
smallest demonstrated limitation, not the entire platform.
