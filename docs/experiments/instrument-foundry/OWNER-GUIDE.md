# Instrument Foundry — owner play guide

Start with the pinned **Instrument Foundry** preview in the publication receipt.
The two reference instruments preserve Descending Resonance and Chime Terraces
exactly. This is a common entrance for the next independent instruments, not a
claim that those familiar worlds have been redesigned.

Try a few shots in either reference. Move origin as well as aim and power. Keep
anything that gives you a useful gesture; it need not be a long phrase. Choose
it as a companion, move to the other instrument, and answer it with a live shot.
A second companion is optional. **None** in both slots gives ordinary solo play.
Selecting a recording leaves your current physical world alone; **Restore this
physical setup** is the separate action that changes it.

The collapsed **Author audition** panel accepts an instrument JSON by file or
paste. A valid file becomes a selectable instrument for this visit, using the
same controls, sounds, Keep and ensemble tools. Download the instrument itself
separately from any performances you want to keep. Reload clears imported
instruments, while saved performances retain their complete physical snapshots.
Loading any instrument or saved ensemble stays silent until an explicit action.

The starter download contains the contract, ready-to-send brief, two references
and an editable scaffold. You can give the same packet to each independent model.
The scaffold is a renamed reference to begin editing, not a third new instrument.

## Two optional, separate visits

**Across the crown** asks how contact position on a broad curve changes a return
or carry. Try low approaches and higher arcs, then compare **Receiving form** in
**About the yard & controls** with the flat control. Survey shows the whole
relationship. This is an ordinary physical study with separate Keeps; it is not
a Counterline instrument and its recordings cannot join your ensemble.

**Exchange Yard roof repair** preserves the canopy and all physical layouts.
In its roof comparator, compare **Corrected triangle draw · current shadows**
with **PR89 · PCF and bias** from the same camera, then try the other views and
full-world context. The draw-path defect is verified; the complete visible repair
still needs your working WebGL browser. No new Freight region was added.

## What would be useful to notice

- Does a newly authored instrument suggest a next change without a shot recipe?
- Can a small or sparse gesture contribute something useful to your ensemble?
- Does a curve offer understandable contact-position choices, or mainly waiting?
- Do the old roof slopes and gables remain visible and stable above, near and
  beneath their edges in the corrected recipe?

Successful numerical settings are kept in probe files and technical appendices,
not in this invitation. There is no score or required sequence here.
