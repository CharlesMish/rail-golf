# Check the old roofs

The new candidate makes the old gabled roofs draw their corrected faces. The
maintenance canopy and every physical surface remain unchanged.

Enter Exchange Yard and revisit a familiar journey. Notice the broad roof
slopes, both gable ends and the eaves, especially when looking down from above.
The key question is whether the see-through appearance and moving dark patches
have disappeared. They need not be the same problem.

If useful, open **Compare roof rendering** from the Yard's About section.
Compare **Corrected triangle draw · current shadows** with **PR89 · PCF and
bias**, keeping the viewpoint and scene context the same. Start in the full
yard, then try the isolated hall. The two original-shadow recipes provide a
second comparison of the same draw correction under the earlier filter.

The older diagnostic variants are preserved. Two-sided rendering was a useful
clue, but ordinary play now retains culling. The underlying draw-path mistake is
verified; complete visual repair still needs a working WebGL renderer. No
research-only shadow or geometry controls are added to ordinary firing.
