## Validation versus reproduction

Schema validation establishes internal consistency; it cannot independently
prove where a file originated. The user supplies its status as genuine owner
play evidence. Fresh equivalent 120 Hz Havok replay separately reproduces all
three complete physical records: setup, launch, all contact rows, every sampled
path point, recognition events, terminal and musical performance. Their uploaded
bytes remain unchanged. Captured acoustic policies also validate against their
recorded normal speeds and gains.

That result is scoped to these three sources under equivalent initialization.
The inherited Descending seam/construction-history limitation remains; it is not
a universal bit-exact replay guarantee after arbitrary world-switch histories.
The source summaries include the independent fresh comparison for each slot.
No listening or new enjoyment result is inferred from numerical agreement. The
owner's explicit report of enjoying the ensemble is the human evidence.

Measured implementation and source hashes are recorded in
`reference-evidence.json`; `owner-evidence-summary.json` is the sanitized
technical appendix and contains numerical launch settings.
