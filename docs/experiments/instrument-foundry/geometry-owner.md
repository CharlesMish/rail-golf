# Across the crown — optional owner study

Open `/lab/expressive-crown`. A broad timber crown sits between two low decks.
Aim and fire with the ordinary controls. Watch where the projectile meets the
curve and where it leaves. Try a lower approach and a higher arc; a return to
the same surface can be as interesting as reaching a different one.

The dark ground ends the line. Survey shows the whole arrangement. Stop and
Retry remain available, and Keep preserves this study's physical line.

In **About the yard & controls**, switch **Receiving form** between the crown
and its flat control. Both have the same footprint, height and receiving decks.
Each version has separate memory. Switching reloads the opening setup, so note
your own aim and power if you want to compare a particular personal line.

This is a geometry study with ordinary impact feedback, not another finished
musical instrument. Its Keeps are separate physical-study records and cannot be
selected as Counterline companions. The technical analysis examines actual
musical onset opportunities separately from the sport's redirect rings.

Does the contact position suggest an understandable next attempt? Do repeated
returns leave useful space between attacks, or mostly make you wait? The latter
is a valid reason to prefer a simpler instrument. This study does not need to
become a generic authoring element merely because its collisions work.
