# Exchange — independent admission and physical review

**Admit the original, unchanged instrument for the temporary exhibition.** Its
five pieces support a clear sparse call-and-response relationship, a longer
continuation, isolated notes and a genuinely delayed answer. It does not need
more geometry to justify its place beside the larger submissions. Accessibility
is concentrated in lower launch regions; a robust selected neighborhood is not
the same as every aimed bank strike producing a reply.

## Submitted evidence versus independent results

The author supplies nine probes, an actual-Havok validator report, a design note
and reported desktop/390-pixel rendered inspection. Their archive checkout had
no git client, so the submitted report says `sourceSha: unknown`; this is disclosed
rather than treated as an independently verified commit. Their note reports
focused tests and TypeScript, but no full suite or listening.

The independent authoritative instrument/probe validators accept the exact data.
All **79 fired settings** reproduce the submitted **full physical payload hashes
exactly**, including contacts, sampled path, recognition, terminal and musical
performance. The two boundary neighbors remain rejected and unclamped. All seven
source-file hashes in the submitted validator report match this baseline. The
independent results therefore strengthen the supplied physical claims; the
originally reported pixels and audible experience are not established by this
headless reproduction.

Files:

- `exchange-validator.json`: complete new authoritative validator output.
- `exchange-independent.json`: original fingerprints, comparison rows, coverage,
  joint sensitivity, construction histories, camera estimates and resources.
- `scripts/exhibition/measure-exchange.mjs`: reproducible independent measurement.
- `original-intake.zip`: director-preserved original submission and evidence.

The new script only reads archive members as data; it does not execute supplied
programs. Its measured runtime file hashes are retained. All stepping is actual
Havok at 120 Hz, unchanged 1.5 kg/.43 m projectile, gravity12, launch mapping and
first-ground termination. No trajectory is predicted or repaired.

selected access is easy to repeat once known, but not broad across the whole range.

regions are accessed without making the overall grid uniformly easier. The
coarse grid finds few exchanges even though the authored opening and nearby
axis/corner tests retain them. This is a useful limitation to take into owner play,
not a reason to replace the author's five-piece idea with a denser arrangement.

Every positive grid impact also exceeds the descriptive gain .01 floor; no weak
floating-point contact inflates these particular counts. This threshold is only
an analysis aid and is not a playback filter or hearing guarantee.

## Acoustic and ensemble interpretation

The exact authored profile is normal-speed dynamics with `tuned-even` playback.
Its timber-bank timbral specialization applies to the far middle bank: the existing
tuned wood voice, 1.36 trim and 12 dB contact-gain range. The ramps retain canonical
timber timbres and lateral banks canonical metal timbres, but their gains also use
the profile's shared 18 dB even contact curve; they do not retain the canonical
smoothstep gain mapping. Pitches remain C4/E4 on the ramps, D4/G4 on lateral banks
and A4 on the middle bank. The measured gains below come from the actual existing
`counterlineVoicedImpact`/`voiceImpact` path, including this distinction.

Selected initial bank gains are .88–1.00; the near-ramp solo is .34. Sparse does
not mean intrinsically quiet: one ringing bank may remain perceptually prominent.
The delayed ramp-to-middle sequence leaves substantial unquantized space that
another source could answer. This is a plausible ensemble role, not a listening
judgment. All selected probes and the shared grid use at most three attacks in
any conservative 2.4-second window. Their bounded cost leaves useful room for a
denser companion, but arbitrary combinations still require the shared budget
check. The director's separate ensemble/audio lane owns cross-instrument balance,
recorded-source compatibility and actual mix measurements.

largest supporting-axis gap is4.423 m; this is not an exact Euclidean distance,

## Camera and admission boundary

An independent geometric estimate applies the actual camera functions to four
canvas shapes. The authored Overview contains all eight corners of every piece
at each aspect. It contains every selected positive probe's sampled path; some
samples can still be hidden behind a physical bank. The silent high-carry negative
leaves the fixed Overview vertically for much of its path. These are geometric
frustum/ray estimates, not screenshots or adaptive-follow comfort measurements.

At the curated western launch, the initial portrait launch frame clips the east
ramp's centre; Overview exposes the complete relationship. Desktop and landscape
initial launch frames contain all five centres. This is a reason to make Overview
visible and easy to reach, not to silently change the author's camera or opening.
Owner inspection remains necessary, especially following the moving ball into an
opposite-side receiver.

Author/probe validation and the existing11 instrument admission/restore tests
pass. The measurement script passes focused lint without new warnings. Full
repository checks, rendering/listening and exhibition interface verification belong
to the director. There is no engineering reason to revise this original before
owner audition. Preserve its sparse character, both openings and short gestures.
