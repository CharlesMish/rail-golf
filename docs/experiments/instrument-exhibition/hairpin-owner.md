# Hairpin — spoiler-free exhibition note

Hairpin is Fable's unchanged instrument. Two wooden ramps sit beneath a lid that
opens differently across its width; higher metal plates stand behind it.

Start with the author's opening, then change one control. Move along the launch
rail as well as adjusting aim and power. Watch whether a change alters the notes,
the spacing between them, or how the ball leaves the gap. Try **Original opening**
too: it approaches the structure differently.

Use **Overview** to inspect the retained trail from the side when the lid obscures
a return. A short strike or pair is worth keeping alongside a longer roll. Choose
one as a companion, switch to another instrument, and listen for what you want
the next live shot to contribute. Solo remains available by clearing both slots.

The useful question is whether repeated contacts make you want to try a different
response, or mostly keep you waiting. Both reactions are valuable. The exhibition
preserves the author's geometry and acoustic interpretation; admission is an
invitation to play, not a permanent repertoire decision.

Numerical recipes and sensitivity findings are in the separate technical report.
