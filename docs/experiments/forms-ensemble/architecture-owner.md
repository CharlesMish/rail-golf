# Exchange Yard: owner invitation

Revisit Forecourt first. The crossing, hall and gallery remain where you knew them. The new maintenance canopy sits across from the small signal house, in the near court to the right as you look out from Forecourt.

Its roof has a curved, faceted crown and a flat underside supported on four piers. All of that substantial structure is physical. The space underneath is open; the dark street still ends a shot. See what the canopy's side and top suggest at different powers, or whether it can send a line toward another part of the works.

Then choose Freight and look back through the district. The canopy may receive a carry or give a roof/crossing journey somewhere else to go. There is no prescribed sequence or score. Keep a line you would like to revisit; the glowing rings still mark substantial qualified rebounds.

Under **About the yard & controls → Physical yard**, choose **Refined** to compare the previous Forecourt/Freight world, or **Original** for Forecourt/Service. Their shelves remain separate. Changing a layout never fires a shot.

The roof shadow treatment is still a candidate. Use **Compare roof rendering** in About to compare it with the previous rendering and the diagnostic variants. We could not certify rendered roof or canopy appearance in the research browser. Please distinguish a useful physical addition from a visual repair that still needs work.

Useful owner questions: Does this region suggest its own next shot? Do its sloping surfaces have understandable consequences? Does Freight feel more purposeful without losing the earlier journeys? Is the camera comfortable near the canopy and hall?

Exact tested shots are deliberately separated into `architecture-solutions.md`.
