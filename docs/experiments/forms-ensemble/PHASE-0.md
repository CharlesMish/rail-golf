## Selection rules

The new region must give Freight another understandable destination or useful
continuation, while retaining valuable Forecourt paths. Contact count is not a
quality target. A curved silhouette is worthwhile only if its playable surfaces
have clear collision authority and acceptable seams/cost. An unconvincing form
may stay a separate diagnostic rather than crowd the Yard.

The trio must preserve separate physical histories and captured/null acoustic
policies, including cross-world selection, eviction/import and silent restore.
No simultaneous companion projectiles. Voice/tail capacity and gain are explicit
bounded policies, with visible failure instead of dropped physical notes. Solo
and duet remain available. No tempo grid, transport, steering or new instrument
is required to establish the ensemble interaction.

Preserve production and all existing research refs/previews. Publication uses
new branches and draft PRs only. Exact endpoint SHAs, trees, tests and pinned
previews are recorded in the final handoff and PR descriptions.
