# Architectural Forms & Ensemble — director handoff

The useful next step is to deepen these two activities independently. Exchange
Yard gains a coherent receiving place; Counterline gains one additional remembered
performance. Neither needs more districts, instruments or editor controls yet.
The owner's enjoyment of the previous Yard and cross-world duet is the human
evidence motivating this work. The new candidates still need owner play.

## Selected architectural result

The maintenance canopy occupies x 14…36 m, z 4…24 m, opposite the signal house.
It is a 22-by-20 m receiving roof supported on four piers, with an open passage
below, a flat 5 m soffit and a 9 m barrel crown. It uses one convex prism with
ten planar roof strips, constructed from the exact visible vertices. It is
honest faceted geometry, not a smooth visual curve hiding a flat collider, and
not a hollow arch. This remains code-owned architecture rather than a general
world-import capability.

Freight gains another destination and continuations after existing roof/viaduct
journeys. A same-footprint flat roof also supports much of that benefit. The
barrel form earns its complexity through a different Forecourt departure toward
the station hall: all six ordinary neighboring adjustments retain it, while the
flat control returns the same central shot to the street. A second Forecourt
continuation survives four of six neighbors. A longer Freight transfer survives
only one of six; it is an optional fragile discovery, not a starting recipe.

All nine named Forecourt control records remain exact. Eight of ten Freight
records remain exact; two retain their earlier contacts and then reach the new
receiver. Changed geometry therefore has a new identity and separate memory.
The previous two complete layouts remain selectable. There is no new station,
larger bounds, projectile rule or scoring objective.

Matched 294-setting grids per station show a modest increase in raised-contact
shots: Forecourt 99→120 and Freight 131→137. The flat control reaches 122 and 138.
These are sampled opportunities, not discovery rates or quality scores. The
canopy supports a reason to choose a different line, rather than winning a
coverage contest. Its cost is five static bodies, five meshes/submeshes and five
casters; solid triangles rise from 240 to 328. Detailed misses, edge normals,
cleanup and camera audits are in [the architectural report](architecture-report.md).

## Roof defect: bounded candidate, unresolved diagnosis

Inspection confirms outward roof buffers and internal coplanarity where roof
bases meet building tops. It also finds a plausible shadow self-interaction
risk in the incumbent blurred exponential filter with near-zero bias. Neither
fact identifies the cause of every owner-reported symptom.

The selected Yard-only candidate uses the already established PCF/low-quality
recipe with bounded depth and normal bias. Roof culling, receivers, casters,
vertices and colliders remain unchanged. Twenty-three complete before/after
actual-Havok histories are exact. No production renderer settings change.

The research browser still cannot initialize WebGL. This is explicitly **not a
claim that flicker, black patches or missing surfaces are fixed**. The seven-way
comparator tests filtering, bias, culling, roof reception and internal cap overlap
independently, in isolated and full-yard context. The [roof report](roof-report.md)
and [owner comparison](roof-owner.md) preserve a useful next diagnostic step.

## Selected musical result

Two independent recorded phrases accompany one live shot, using one common
audio origin and the existing 100 ms scheduling buffer. The four musical worlds,
authored pitches, acoustic profiles and physical identities remain unchanged.
World switching retains companion choices; physical Restore is a separate act.
Solo and duet remain ordinary options.

The additive `rail-golf-counterline-ensemble` v1 record stores three independent
physical sources, their explicit captured-or-historical acoustic interpretations,
and per-layer gain/mute settings. Nothing turns the companions into another
projectile or a combined physical ledger. Old Keeps, pairs and Contact Loops
remain compatible. A separate four-arrangement library preserves source copies
without enlarging or rewriting the historical four-Keep shelf.

Only complete uninterrupted performances can become new saved ensembles. A
physical shot ending naturally can leave companions and tails playing. Stop,
Retry, source/world change, focus loss, suspension and stale asynchronous audio
preparation cancel playback. Loading is silent; audition is explicitly recorded
playback. Active level edits are disabled; muting cuts that layer and invalidates
a falsely fixed new arrangement while preserving the actual physical line.

The scheduler admits at most 36 sounding attacks with overlapping conservative
2.4-second lifetimes. Unsupported known density fails before playback; unexpected
live overflow stops explicitly instead of dropping notes. Default levels are 30%
per part, bounded at 45%. This is a bounded ensemble experiment, not a guarantee
that every possible imported dense combination will play.

All 252 equivalent actual-Havok comparisons match their complete physical payload
under solo, duet and trio, across four worlds and three simulated frame partitions.
Actual browser Web Audio probes schedule all 30 dense-case or 10 mixed-case attacks
at their original target times, with no late attacks and zero active voices at
completion. Offline dense peaks are .868 at 30% and .891 at 45%; the latter reaches
the existing limiting ceiling. These establish bounded technical behavior, not
comfortable loudness or musical enjoyment. Hardware output latency and listening
were not verified. See [integration](ensemble-integration.md),
[physical evidence](ensemble-physics.md) and [browser verification](VERIFICATION.md).

## Design judgment and next evidence

The canopy is technically demonstrated and code-authorable. Nearby control
variation supports expressive departures; discoverability, rendered readability
and new enjoyment remain owner questions. Archive the addition if it feels like
an obligatory extra bounce rather than a place deliberately revisited.

The ensemble is technically demonstrated and preserves independent authorship.
It makes the owner's existing interpretation of worlds as instruments directly
usable. Whether three parts inspire a meaningful live answer is unresolved.
Keep duet prominent if a second companion mainly obscures the new performance.

Prioritize at most two capabilities next:

1. **A bounded faceted receiving form**, only if this canopy's distinct departures
   prove worth returning to. It does not justify arbitrary meshes or a 3D editor.
2. **Portable independent musical phrases with a small accompaniment budget**,
   before adding another layer, tempo grid or new sound engine.

For a future independent multi-model authoring round, invite short, approachable
physical instruments that leave useful empty intervals and offer at least two
broad timing families. Ask authors to demonstrate a different answer to the same
retained phrase, without prescribing contact counts. The current repertoire
already supplies separated and clustered material in a shared C4/D4/E4/G4/A4
register; do not claim existing worlds are bass or percussion instruments merely
from their names. Commission an extra register or timbre only if owner listening
identifies that missing role.

Independent review found and helped close source-policy, capacity and lifecycle
boundary problems, including provisional audition retaining an unfinished trio.
Its [report and reproducible probes](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) recommend retaining
these two candidates while leaving the rendered roof issue open. Production,
prior research branches and previews remain outside this publication.
