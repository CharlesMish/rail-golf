## Interface and persistence observations

Actual browser interactions loaded an ensemble silently without changing live
world/aim, saved four arrangements, reloaded with 4/4 retained and both companions
empty, and confirmed the initial “Silent until Fire” status. All saved source
copies remained selectable with zero physical Keeps. Recorded audition disabled
level changes; Stop preserved its already completed record. The final pending-
live save/audition guards were independently source-reviewed; rendered live-fire
exercise was unavailable.

CSS viewports 1280×600,1024×480,390×844 and 844×390 used the actual pages in a
temporary iframe harness at unchanged browser zoom. With four saved ensembles
and expanded provenance, document/main horizontal overflow was zero at every
size. Keyboard navigation from the last arrangement's Remove control reached
the Stop control visibly; main scrolling remained available. Measured ensemble
canvas sizes were 730×390,572×330,349×397 and 803×290 respectively.

The roof comparator and expanded Yard About controls also had zero horizontal
overflow at all four sizes. Keyboard focus visibly reached Return to Exchange
Yard and Compare roof rendering. Yard version selection navigated to Refined,
then Original with its Service station, then Canopy. Graphics-dependent controls
were correctly unavailable. These are DOM, layout and keyboard observations,
not a visual inspection of projectile or architecture. The temporary harness
was removed before the production build and is not published.

## Explicit limits

WebGL could not initialize in this research browser, including the unchanged
control. No roof image, shadow comparison, live 3D camera, draw-call measurement,
GPU-memory test or physical-phone frame-time evidence was obtained. The browser
does run Web Audio, so audio measurements above are genuine and separately scoped.
No NullEngine timing is described as rendered performance. The five-body canopy
and the PCF recipe remain candidates for owner renderer/performance confirmation.

The described owner screenshots and Trading Legend illustration were not among
the accessible attachments; their contents were not invented. The unresolved
roof diagnosis and the need for actual ensemble listening are substantive limits,
not reasons to replace the otherwise verified physical experiments with claims
of finished visual or musical quality.
