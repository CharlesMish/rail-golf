# Ensemble: physical authority, opportunity and bounded audio evidence

The ensemble remains one actual projectile beside zero, one or two recorded
histories. This measurement does not give the recorded companions a physics
body, change a world document, or retime a contact. The parent implementation
report describes the UI, independent arrangement format and storage contract.

Foundation: independent Counterline repertoire PR85,
`6f080b78efb5afbeb5d8d087b1426e5681d6726d`.
Measured ensemble implementation: `06d48a399ca77a56f32b76a0a3898462517065c4`.
Reproduce with `node scripts/forms-ensemble/ensemble-measure.mjs` and
`node --test tests/counterline-ensemble.test.mjs`. The concise raw packet is
`ensemble-evidence.json`; it contains exact source SHA/file hashes, setups,
terminal outcomes, original contact histories and path/complete-payload hashes.
Numerical launch settings belong to that technical packet, not the owner guide.

## Same shot, different accompaniment

Four unchanged worlds were checked: Descending Resonance, Chime Terraces, the
early-plate Workshop and the original Workshop. Each received seven ordinary
setups: arrival, neighboring yaw on both sides, a lower elevation detent, one
power detent higher, a low edge miss and a high carry. Every setup was compared
against its own actual-Havok 120 Hz control under all three accompaniment counts
and three rendered-frame partitions: 30 Hz, 144 Hz and a mixed
144/30/approximately-14/60 Hz sequence. These are simulated frame partitions,
not measured browser frame rates.

**252/252 comparisons match the complete physical payload exactly:** world,
setup, launch, contact ledger, sampled trajectory, recognized evidence, terminal
and resonance history. The audio clock is a deterministic fake context in this
script. It exercises the actual ensemble scheduler, including live collision
notifications, but produces no sound. There were zero late scheduled attacks or
scheduler interruptions in these comparisons. Eighteen representative mode/frame
combinations also have direct deep-equality assertions in the focused tests.

The new integration therefore preserves the committed-shot authority under the
measured equivalent construction and time-stepping conditions. It does not
resolve the inherited Descending shared-surface construction-history discrepancy
reported in `../refinement/counterline-physics.md`. Different initialization
histories can still change some seam-sensitive physical outcomes.

The selected Descending arrival provides separated strikes at approximately

contrasting material, not permanent instrument classifications.

The high carries are six-second silent physical flights, not successful musical
phrases. The Terraces lower-elevation neighbor ends out of bounds after five
sounds. Neither is repaired by adding accompaniment. A silent new physical shot
can nevertheless be retained as its own independent ensemble source; no note is
fabricated for it.

## Capacity and balance

The bounded scheduler permits **36 attacks whose 2.4-second conservative voice
lifetimes overlap**, up from the paired scheduler's 24. The overlap calculation
uses an actual sliding window; a centered window would incorrectly reject
separated clusters. A dedicated regression distinguishes those cases and tests
the exact 2.4-second boundary.

All 64 ordered trios made from the four archived richer-world captures fit the
budget; their maximum is 12 overlapping attacks. A denser **actual Havok**
Terraces history has ten sounding contacts. Repeating that recorded source in
all three slots is a deliberate load test, not a claim of an appealing
arrangement: it schedules **30 attacks**, all at their original targets, and
finishes with no pending playback. Its captured fixture is
`public/forms-ensemble/ensemble-examples.json`, ready for actual browser offline
and clock probes. Two synthetic over-dense sources are rejected explicitly;
when live contacts cause a later overflow, all playback stops with an explanation
rather than silently dropping notes.

The H timber voice has five oscillators plus noise, while canonical metal has
four plus noise and canonical timber has three plus noise. Thus 36 supported
attacks imply a conservative maximum of 216 scheduled source nodes before natural
endings, plus the existing gains/filter/mix infrastructure. This is an analytic
upper bound, not a measured browser-active-node count. Existing resonance uses a
shared limiting bus; that does not establish comfortable perceived loudness.
Browser offline peak/RMS and real AudioContext lifecycle observations belong in
the director's verification packet.

New ensemble layers begin at 30% and are bounded to 45%; historical pair imports
retain their explicit 45% setting. Gain changes require Stop or completion, so
unrecorded mid-phrase automation cannot masquerade as a fixed saved mix. Muting
releases that layer's scheduled voices; unmuting affects future contacts only.

## Focused contract checks

**Fourteen new tests pass.** They cover independent immutable sources and acoustic policies,
historical pair compatibility, bounds and tamper rejection, shared unquantized
times and per-layer gains, both natural ending orders, mute isolation, explicit
budget failures, stale asynchronous unlock, twelve repeated Stop/restart cycles,
suspension/stalls/late events/scheduler failure, actual Havok equality, silent
physical-source preservation, bounded four-arrangement storage and concurrent
write refusal. Recorded-only audition also applies its own saved mix after an
unrelated audio preparation. Corrupt existing bytes remain untouched. Browser rendering and
musical enjoyment are intentionally outside these assertions.

## Next instrument-authoring question

The current repertoire already supplies separated phrases, clusters and sparse
answers. A further model-authored instrument should be commissioned only after
listening reveals a missing role. One promising brief would ask for **short,
approachable rhythmic replies with useful empty intervals**, using existing
physical and acoustic capabilities, with at least two broad timing families.
Do not prescribe a target contact count or call a world percussion solely because
it contains timber. Compare actual histories, then ask whether players choose it
for a different musical intention. No additional instrument was built here.
