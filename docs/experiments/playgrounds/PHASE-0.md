# Next Playgrounds — director triage

Started 21 November 2026 from remote refs verified below. This document records
the selection before complete worlds were commissioned; lane reports distinguish
later measured results. The original eighteen-concept document was read in full
(`Pasted text(4).txt`, 85,243 bytes), alongside the new commission and Horizons
reports. It was recovered from the owner's files, not inferred from the brief's
short summaries.

## What changed after the owner's Horizons playtest

Swing Gate was entertaining but needed consequences in a larger place. Overpass
was a useful ingredient. Crossing House was too sparse and familiar. Radial
Return was too small and close to the launcher. These are owner observations;
the previous reports' unknown-enjoyment entries are historical, not current
evidence that those tests were never played.

The selection therefore favors different activities: composing appearances,
learning an enclosing place, and using one shot to arrange the next. Local
robustness remains necessary evidence, not a substitute for those activities.

## The eighteen proposals

| Proposal | Experience to preserve | Dependency and triage |
|---|---|---|
| Meridian Stepwell | A descent with choices around a substantial well | 120 m size, 30 m depth, gnomons, bridge and arbitrary solids are not ordinary Foundry data. Borrow architectural ambition; reject assumed wall-riding and a contact-count continuation gate. |
| Barrow's Cairn | Shots construct the next route | Multiple unstable stacks, persistent transforms and undo are several commitments. Test one persistent body first; no rockslide system. |
| Drift-Ice Caldera | Carry across responsive islands | Buoyancy, custom contact materials, drift and state are independent risks. Defer; a one-floe proof would still need several new physical authorities. |
| Lockhouse Terraces | Choose elevated channels and crosswise transfers | Broad arbitrary slabs, raised station and bevel joins exceed the fixed catalog. Inform the architectural proof; do not presume a rolling transfer. |
| Assay Hall | Arrival speed changes a passive mechanism's response | A constrained beam is a reasonable future proof, but sustained ball dwell and mass-ratio behavior are unproven. Defer the five-beam hall. |
| Skipping Cloister | Select skipping versus plunging | Requires a new water-contact rule plus separate buoyant bodies. Do not fake a skip by correcting velocities after an ordinary collision. Defer. |
| Dry Stepwell | Ordinary misses stay within useful architecture | 90 m width, 35 m depth and 50–60 bodies are not established Foundry capability. Retain enclosure and regional choices in a bounded segment. |
| Low-Tide Signal Stacks | Earn viewpoints by coming to rest | Rest, safe muzzle placement, origin persistence, camera and progression all need contracts. Strong future direction; no arbitrary settled-point launcher in this portfolio. |
| Salt-Crust Cenote | Carve an opening, then use it | Honest punch-through requires pre-resolution collision control; removing a body after bounce is insufficient. Defer fracture and falling props. |
| Ringwell | Learn a room with several reachable regions | Selected spatial direction, conditional on broad contacts and useful exits. Use code-owned solids and existing elevated station support; do not call it ordinary JSON authoring. |
| Vesper Saddle | Choose a basin through landing position and carry | Selected bounded wildcard proof. Nonterminal obstacles already exist; continuity, route selection and stable contact are the actual unknowns. |
| Guest Stone | Spend shots relocating one awkward occupant | Selected physical comparison with the dock. Preserve free-body character if movements are controllable and settling is short. |
| Tin Roofs | Read roof pitches and cross streets | Cheap pair-of-roofs proof first. Exit depends on incoming velocity and normal, not pitch alone. No village before transfer evidence. |
| Low Water | Trade exposed routes against higher launch access | Worth a separate future experiment. Terminal plane, tee, saved identity and Restore are one contract; compare matched controls and matched global launch separately. |
| Raked Court | Compose with actual endings | Preserve as author outcome-map inspiration initially. A human-sized session may reveal little structure; automated maps are not proof of player composition. |
| Market Before Opening | Learn differently oriented neighborhoods | Selected roof-transfer comparison; retain the orientation insight even if the developed spatial world becomes a cistern. |
| Transfer Dock | Prepare a reflector position through impact | Compare one passive constrained carriage with Guest Stone; do not assume the constrained option is automatically better. |
| Two-Shadow Theatre | Make one real flight tell two visual stories | Selected. Fixed projections and shared time/scale can use recorded Havok positions without new forces or imported scripts. Keep it free of scores. |

## Distinct costs

| Selected question | Minimum proof | Playable commitment | Generic authoring commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theatre | Fixed-step path projected onto two perpendicular planes | A small stage, purposeful broad contacts, voluntary Keep, paired comparison and synchronized inspection | Standard path provenance, recording bounds, stable projection conventions and UI across many worlds; not needed for this bespoke study |
| Architectural transfers | Two roofs and/or two ledges, outgoing vectors and a receiving-surface control | Several broad accessible regions with recovery possibilities and a readable whole-place camera | Bounded solid dimensions, placement/overlap checks, station clearance, camera guidelines; substantially beyond making code-owned boxes |
| Persistent object | One body, left/right/central impacts, settled state and exact restoration | Two useful parking regions, recovery access, explicit settling/Undo/Reset and pre/post records | Validated dynamic templates, collision clearance, deterministic reset, rest policy and safe import contracts; no arbitrary rigid-body editor |
| Carrying face | One ridge versus a flat/sloped control | Only justified by broad, distinguishable exits rather than a few spectacular edge contacts | Surface tessellation contract, seams/normals, material bounds and rest behavior; no terrain sculpting this session |

- Radial Return: `00110e6890a0cc9541c9047a20c91d9670b206ee`.

The new lanes use the verified Horizons base for its accepted spatial-session,
recording and optional UI foundations. Custom elevated stations already work in
that session; the amphitheater's curved geometry and Worldcraft station schema
are unnecessary dependencies. No new lane is based on PR79 or PR78. New shapes
remain code-owned lab geometry, not silently expanded Worldcraft imports.

Stop expansion if regional choices mostly reduce to one draining sequence,
ordinary contacts depend on narrow edges, persistence only gives negligible
motion or end-stop travel, or a visual study hides a difference through changing
scale. Do not substitute more decoration, a larger sweep, or a contact quota.

Fusion starts deferred. It must earn a distinct choice compared with its parts;
three separate legible activities are preferable to one overloaded installation.
An author-only outcome atlas may expose unused geometry and reachable surface
fans, but it will not rank world quality or prescribe what every author builds.
