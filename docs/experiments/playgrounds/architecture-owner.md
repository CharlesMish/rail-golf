# Gallery Cistern — owner invitation

An open room with high and low galleries and a side chamber. See where a long fall can take you.

Start from the overview. The pale walls and colored stone platforms all collide; the dark floor and launch pier end a shot. The chamber on the left is a real connected space, not scenery.

You can move between the three launch positions and change direction, elevation and power. Fire once, then choose a different part of the room to visit. Try approaching one place by another route. Keep a line if it matters to you; Restore returns its setup so that you can fire it again.

There is no required route, contact quota or score. A missed gallery, an escape over the room, or a return you did not expect can be useful information. The short way across and the long way around need not feel like the same question.

If a shot stops being interesting, use Stop/Retry. Some lines can spend too long bouncing and settling on stone; you do not need to watch them finish.

After a few minutes, tell us which part of the room made you want another shot, whether you could understand what changed, and which part you wanted to reach but could not. Please also report if the overview, ball or controls obscure the relationship you are trying to explore.

This is a physical prototype. The geometry and recorded lines have been verified with Havok; rendered desktop/phone visibility and human enjoyment remain to be established in a WebGL-enabled browser. Numerical solutions and comparisons are deliberately separated in [the research appendix](architecture-appendix.md).
