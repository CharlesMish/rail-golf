# Exchange Yard — an invitation

Enter `/lab/places-architecture` from the Places & Counterlines preview.

A railway crosses a small district of copper roofs, loading galleries and service courts. The Forecourt looks into the crossing; the Service lane looks across the works. Both stations are available immediately.

- Start with the Forecourt. Look for a relationship between the low passage, the railway deck and the farther buildings. Change one control and watch what becomes reachable.
- Try the Service lane when you want a different view of the same place. Its open court faces the station and the edge of the loading gallery.
- Use Survey to read the district, then Follow to watch a committed shot. Stop is available if a line has finished being interesting.
- Keep any completed line you want to remember. Restore returns its original station and setup; firing remains your choice.

The dark street ends a shot. Raised architecture, including the pitched roofs, has matching physical collision geometry. Fine tracks, windows and brick courses are painted markings. There is no target, unlocking requirement or contact-count goal.

Useful owner questions: Does the place make you want to visit another region? Do the two stations invite different ideas? Does a nearby change have a consequence you can understand? Is following a roof or underpass journey comfortable at ordinary zoom?
