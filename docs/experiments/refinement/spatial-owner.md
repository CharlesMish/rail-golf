# Exchange Yard — owner invitation

The district is physically the same place you enjoyed. The near faces of its roofs have been corrected, and the ordinary shot controls are visible beside the world.

Start at **Forecourt** and revisit a familiar route. The structures and physical response are unchanged. Then choose **Freight dock**: you are standing on the existing dock, looking back through the district. Try treating the crossing as a receiver, explore the nearby station side, or ask where a roof might send the ball next. A small change of elevation or power can change which level receives it.

The original Forecourt/Service version remains available as a comparison. Its saved lines stay with that version. Keep anything you would like to revisit; changing versions will not reinterpret an old line in a different station setup.

The rings still mark qualified physical redirects. They are optional visual feedback, not targets or a score. Dark street still ends the shot; raised architecture is physical. Fine track and building details are painted markings.

Useful owner questions:

- Does Freight suggest a different next shot, rather than only another way to reach familiar objects?
- Can you aim, adjust power, fire and retry comfortably at ordinary zoom?
- Do all roof faces look solid from Launch, Survey and flight views?
- Does the Freight camera make a return easy to watch, especially near the piers and loading gallery?

The research browser could not render WebGL. Mesh orientation, actual Havok contacts and geometric camera checks pass, but visual appearance and camera comfort still need your playtest. Numerical examples are kept in the separate technical appendix.
