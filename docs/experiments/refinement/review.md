# Independent refinement review

This review is separate from the spatial, interface and musical implementation lanes. The common starting authority is PR #84, `8b02b8ceab95f28383f607cc4e891fe7e31dfc7a`. Charlie's playtest is the current design authority: Exchange Yard is already enjoyable after over a hundred shots, whereas Counterline's small workshops did not offer enough accessible musical variety to judge accompaniment fairly.

## Acceptance boundaries

- Repair roof presentation without changing the existing successful collision geometry. An inward-normal diagnosis is not a rendered-lighting certification.
- Preserve Forecourt relationships. Select a second station only for a different useful relationship with existing architecture; greater sampled contact coverage is insufficient.
- Bring normal aim, power, Fire, station and recovery controls forward without importing research clutter into the ordinary shooting loop.
- Bring the original registered musical documents into Counterline. Do not replace their physical identities, pitches or fixed rules with undocumented copies.
- Preserve canonical legacy audio gain and timing. A historical gain cannot be assumed to encode face speed; unrecorded voice choices must remain explicitly unknown.
- Keep new acoustic metadata separate from the immutable v1 physical line. Pairings contain two real histories; only the new projectile enters Havok.

## Baseline independently established

All 17 original focused Counterline and Exchange Yard tests pass in the review checkout. Those tests include actual Havok reproducibility, both station records, qualified contact recognition, historical pair authority and interruption cleanup.

The independent [roof probe](review-probe.mjs) examines the actual flat-shaded mesh rather than relying on a hand-drawn winding convention. All 24 roof triangles (three prisms, eight triangles each) have normals pointing toward their interior centroid. Babylon's stock box is the positive-orientation control. Reversing triangle winding gives outward normals; disabling back-face culling would hide the defect rather than fix it. The original nine retained physical histories replay exactly, and all owned world bodies, meshes, materials, textures, geometry and casters dispose to zero. [Original evidence](review-original-roof-evidence.json).

The existing Counterline route forces face-speed dynamics and a single workshop opening, while `performanceFromResonance` retains gain but omits dynamics, face speed and the selected bank voice. The renderer's `voiceImpact` can infer face speed from a stored PR55 gain; applying that inference to an arbitrary old total-speed line is unjustified. Descending Resonance explicitly selects face-speed dynamics and H tuned timber banks. Chime Terraces explicitly selects face-speed dynamics and ordinary voices. These different authored policies must be represented without reinterpreting unannotated historical records.

The original Descending opening is a robust **single** E4. Its existence does not by itself resolve Charlie's one-note frustration. A clearly labelled alternate launch starting condition can help expose a richer family while keeping the original document and authored opening intact. Exact settings belong in the numerical appendix; the invitation can describe ramps, hanging plates, separated strikes and clustered contacts.

## Design review guidance

A reverse Freight station at dock height is a promising modest alternative: the launch remains below the viaduct receiving surface and approaches the buildings from the other side. Its evidence should demonstrate where the ball goes after its first contact, and which intention differs from Forecourt. A much higher station risks repeating Cistern's elevated-launch problem. An additional building is unnecessary unless a specific observed gap demands one.

Timber Courtyard's transferable interface quality is its direct aiming and firing emphasis, readable charge meter and short feedback. Copying every inherited disclosure would retain the problem: production itself hides some set-power controls behind Shot Tools. Exchange Yard is allowed to put those controls in plain view, with history and diagnostics secondary. Charging must still cancel safely on interruption; station changes must preserve the player's current aim before clearing the last result.

### Important inherited replay limitation

The survey identified, and this reviewer independently reproduced, **construction-history sensitivity at touching surfaces in Descending Resonance**. A fresh scene repeats exactly, but constructing other worlds first can change a simultaneous ramp or bank contact. One ramp-seam example changes the later route and terminal destination, not only the displayed contact order. A selected separated descent remains byte-identical across both histories.

The original 875-shot survey is reproduced exactly after its historical Terraces → Fan → Garden construction order. A fresh Descending scene matches 860/875 rows; the rich world's documents and coefficients are unchanged. Chime Terraces matches all 875 historical rows from a fresh scene. This is a real limit on exact re-firing after arbitrary world switches. It is not evidence that the adapter changed the authored geometry, nor justification to tune the solver or move historical pieces. Preserve the actual saved path and identify this replay limit honestly.
