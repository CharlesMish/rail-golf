# Exchange Yard & Counterline — director handoff

**The strongest refinement is to give the existing places clearer intentions.**
Exchange Yard does not need another district: a reverse-facing dock station
changes what its existing roofs and crossing can do. Counterline does not need
another musical geometry experiment: the registered Descending Resonance and
Chime Terraces already contain much more material than the small workshops.

The owner's hundred-plus Yard shots establish enjoyment of the original place.
This round preserves that architecture and Forecourt. Whether the new station
and presentation improve return visits remains an owner comparison. Counterline's
musical usefulness likewise requires listening; timing, phrase counts and audio
peaks cannot answer it.

## Selected Yard revision

The roof defect was real: all 24 triangles across the three gabled roofs pointed
inward under Babylon's normal convention. The repair reverses render faces and
normals after flattening, preserving the exact convex-hull vertex buffer and
ordering. All 13 retained original physical histories match exactly, including
callbacks and paths. Back-face culling and shadow participation remain enabled.
This is a mathematically verified repair with unchanged collision behavior;
rendered lighting/shadows still need a WebGL inspection.

The new playing interface exposes aim, elevation, rail position, station, power
and Fire beside an unobscured canvas. Timed charging remains optional. Camera,
Stop and Retry are visible during flight; Keep, history and detailed evidence
are secondary. Qualified redirect rings remain. Production and historical lab
interfaces retain their existing presentation.

Freight replaces Service only in a separately versioned yard. It faces back from
the existing dock, below the main roofs and viaduct. No solid, physical material
or bound changed. Forecourt's nine retained examples preserve their physical
outcomes exactly. The original physical world and its shelf remain selectable;
old lines cannot silently restore into the new station definition.

Freight is chosen for different approach and continuation, despite fewer
contacting settings than Service. Roof-to-viaduct and viaduct-to-signal-roof
families each survive six neighboring control detents. Low hall/platform play
uses a different part of the power range. The high station repeats Cistern's
risk: it sits above too many useful receivers, with 11 settled endings and a
52.775-second maximum. No additional structure was justified.

The reversed approach also exposed two camera defects: a launch boom entering
the hall at extreme aim, and an abrupt pier/gallery reframe. Freight-only
presentation changes fix the sampled cases while retaining Forecourt's camera.
The final audit covers 12,513 flight samples and 870 launch views at the actual
new canvas sizes, with no sampled obstruction or frustum failures. This is
geometric evidence, not a rendered comfort claim.

## Counterline with a fairer repertoire

Both richer worlds load through the existing Foundry registry and validators.
Geometry, assigned pitches, capacity, physical identity and original openings
are unchanged. The new adapter respects each world's controls and authored
acoustics. Clearly labelled **Falling phrase** and **Rising chimes** arrival
setups use existing tested shots; both retain at least three separated onset
events under all eight tested neighboring changes. Original opening remains a
silent, explicit choice.

The diagnostic groups successive onsets separated by at most 150 ms; playback
retains every original timestamp. These are sampled outcomes, not discovery
rates. Descending offers more separated phrases; Terraces also offers clusters.
Short single strikes and silence remain legitimate musical choices.

Descending uses its authored H tuned timber-bank interpretation; Terraces keeps
canonical plate/ramp voices. A small acoustic envelope records the selected
profile and measured face speeds separately from unchanged Worldcraft v1 physical
lines. Pair v2 retains two independent sources and per-source acoustic policies.
Pair v1 and older Keeps preserve stored gains and canonical voices. Unknown old
timbre is disclosed; total-speed records are never silently converted to H.
Physical-only export remains compatible with Contact Loops.

The companion still contributes zero Havok bodies and zero live ledger entries.
The established shared clock and 100 ms buffer remain. Captured H voices receive
their full longer tail; legacy timing remains unchanged. Browser recorded-audio
probes scheduled all 20 attacks across three rich pairings at their intended
times, with no late events and zero surviving voices. These measurements do not
certify live rendered physics/audio feel or musical quality.

**Inherited limitation:** fresh Descending construction differs from the archived
survey on 15/875 seam-sensitive rows. Repeating the original world-construction
sequence reproduces all archived rows. A seam case can alter later trajectory
and destination, not merely contact ordering. Terraces reproduces all 875 rows
fresh; the selected Descending descent remains exact across tested histories.
No solver or geometry was changed to conceal the difference. Full records and
negative examples remain in the physical report.

The richer worlds now reuse their established side-result framing. A
Terraces-only lens adjustment fixes four clipped ramp corners at the measured
openings. This checks frustum inclusion, not arbitrary-aim visibility or
occlusion.

## Cost and next decision

Exchange Yard remains 21 static bodies, one active projectile, 24 world meshes
and 20 shadow casters. Fourteen disposal cycles release all counted resources.
Counterline's richer worlds cost 14/23 static bodies and 32/53 ready meshes,
versus the workshops' 5 bodies and 17 meshes. Forty mixed-world cycles preserve
counts and dispose cleanly. These are NullEngine allocation/CPU measurements;
rendered draw calls, frame timing, heap/GPU memory and physical-phone performance
are not established here.

Stop feature expansion for the next playtest. Ask whether Freight changes the
intended next journey, and whether the richer musical material makes answering
a companion more deliberate. A musical Exchange Yard is feasible, but needs a
separate versioned mapping from Spatial Lab contact histories to notes while
preserving spatial station provenance. It should wait for these listening
findings. No generic schema, new district, extra accompaniment layer or new
persistent-object mechanism is warranted by this round alone.
