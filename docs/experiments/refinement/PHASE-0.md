## Director decisions before implementation

- Preserve the original physical Yard as a control. A visual-only roof repair
  must reproduce its actual physical histories. Any changed station definition
  gets a distinct layout identity and cannot consume old Keeps as a new yard.
- Compare station positions against the existing architecture before adding a
  structure. The existing Forecourt has owner value; aggregate coverage is not
  permission to sacrifice its routes. A second station must expose a different
  direction and destination, not simply increase the contact count.
- Give Exchange Yard its own playing presentation. The current console puts
  power/elevation under Shot tools and overlays much of the view. Primary shot
  controls belong beside the scene, with preservation and evidence secondary.
  Historical lab and production layouts remain scoped controls.
- Load Descending Resonance and Chime Terraces through the registered Foundry
  data and validation. Their geometry, notes, opening and presentation remain
  authored authority. Counterline's hardcoded workshop limits are not authority.
- Capture acoustic interpretation additively where necessary. Historical gains
  and timestamps must not be silently reinterpreted as face-speed dynamics, and
  missing historical timbre remains explicitly unknown. Companion audio never
  enters live physics or its contact ledger.
- No new musical world, sequencing system, persistent-object mechanic or generic
  geometry schema is part of this round.
