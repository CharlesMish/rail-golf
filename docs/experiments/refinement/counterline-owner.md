# Counterline: a broader musical repertoire

Choose **Descending Resonance** for raised, leaning surfaces and more separated phrases. Choose **Chime Terraces** for a wider field of metal plates, with ringing clusters as well as individual steps.

Each opens on a labelled starting setup from its existing tested repertoire. **Original opening** is available for comparison. Neither choice changes the world. Small changes in aim, height or power are a good way to hear related material; moving the origin can change the relationship more substantially.

Start in Solo. Fire, listen, and Keep a line that leaves you curious. Choose it as your companion, then try a new shot that answers it, leaves a gap, or adds a contrasting rhythm. One well-placed note is welcome. The companion plays once; only your new shot is physical.

Use the separate mute controls to hear either contribution. Retry and Stop cancel the old pairing. Restore loads a setup silently; Fire is always yours. The two smaller workshops and original solo Worldcraft remain available.

New captured recordings preserve the selected acoustic interpretation. Older Keeps retain their stored dynamics with canonical voices; their original timbre may be unknown. Download recordings/pairings to preserve both physical lines and their musical context. The separate physical export preserves the original Worldcraft v1 memory format and can be imported into Contact Loops. It omits the new acoustic information; the ordinary Worldcraft page does not import memory bundles.

Questions for listening: Does a remembered phrase suggest a specific next gesture? Are separated contacts or ringing clusters easier to answer? Does the sound feel attached to the collision with the shared delay? Do nearby changes feel like variations you want to keep exploring?
