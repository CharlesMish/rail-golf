# Stone Circle — owner play guide

In Worldcraft, open **Foundry → Amphitheater sketches → Stone Circle → Play**. The launcher is in the central space and faces the seating. There is no score or target.

1. Fire the opening shot and follow where the round goes after meeting the courses.
2. Change one control at a time. Try a small yaw or elevation adjustment and compare the resulting lines; then try a different power or origin.
3. Explore more than one part of the seating. Keep a line if something surprises you, and compare it with a revision.
4. Consider whether the place feels inviting, whether the visible surfaces behave as expected, and whether you want another shot. Listen for distinct contacts without expecting a composed melody.

The line ends at first ground, if the ball settles, or when it leaves the yard. This is an experimental build; presentation, sound and camera behavior still need owner play. The [technical appendix](STONE-CIRCLE-APPENDIX.md) contains shot details and spoilers.
