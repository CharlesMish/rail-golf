# Centre Stage

Lab experiment on `/lab/worldcraft`. Open **Foundry**, then **Amphitheater sketches**, then **Centre Stage**, then **Play**.

You are on the stage, in the middle of the timber steps. Straight ahead looks into the curved seating. Play opens the overview, with the rail on the stage. Address is the view from that rail. The line under the aim controls names the station: across, downrange, and the facing. Yaw turns from that facing. Zero is straight ahead. The usual rail stops still apply.

The steps stay where they are. There is no target and no score. The line ends on the first ground, on the tee, or when the ball leaves the yard.

**Down the Aisle** and **From the Stage** are the earlier arrangements of the same steps. They are still in the list.

The aim mark at the muzzle is a short direction mark. It is not a predicted path.

Numbers, the comparison with the earlier bowl, and the shot records are in [PHASE3-APPENDIX.md](PHASE3-APPENDIX.md).
