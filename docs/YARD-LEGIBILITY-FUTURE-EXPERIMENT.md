# Future experiment: reading a persistent physical change

This is a Studio proposal, not part of the yard correction. Keep the current pass's physics, recognition, scoring, storage and historical labs unchanged. Do not promote E8 or another mechanism into production.

## Question

When the same physical change becomes easier to see and recover, do players intentionally use the altered yard—and do they understand it as clearance, a supporting surface, or both?

R6 reports six of six blind agent testers encountered E8's timber, five later contacted treads available from its downed geometry, but only one expressed qualifying intent about the moved piece. Several described an obstruction cleared. Physical success alone cannot establish either interpretation.

## Bounded design

First establish consistent starting-snapshot restoration and current-yard continuation. Then compare matched familiarization followed by ordinary play with identical physics, geometry, controls, state semantics and contact recognition. Vary only a restrained visual treatment that makes the downed timber and its relationship to Tread 1 distinguishable. Avoid naming it “ramp,” showing an ideal trajectory or prescribing a goal.

Preregister separate observations: noticing persistent change; intending clearance; intending support/climb; intentional reuse; successful physical contact. Code plans before outcomes and accept multiple interpretations. Record both environment-at-address and shot-caused change. Distinguish familiarized deliberate use from spontaneous discovery; supplement agent sessions with human play and real-phone inspection before drawing product conclusions.
